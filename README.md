## 🚀 Flagship Build — ARGUS · Offline AI Video Intelligence

<a href="https://github.com/satyamshrivastav955-dotcom/ai-surveillance">
  <img src="./assets/proj-argus.svg" width="100%" alt="ARGUS — offline AI video intelligence: see the scene, understand the event, deliver the alert" />
</a>

**See the scene. Understand the event. Deliver the alert.** ARGUS transforms camera feeds into structured observations and event-driven alerts — running inside constrained local hardware instead of depending on the cloud.

- **Perception pipeline** — YOLO-family detection, ByteTrack tracking, pose-based fall/interaction events, plus configurable fire, smoke, and phone-use detectors.
- **Async VLM reasoning** — a quantized Qwen2.5-VL path produces natural-language scene descriptions without blocking the primary video loop.
- **Offline-first alerting** — structured event logging, keyframe capture, and nearby-device alert delivery over a Bitchat/Android link.
- **Companion study** — a separate [argus-vlm-optimization](https://github.com/satyamshrivastav955-dotcom/argus-vlm-optimization) repo benchmarks temporal visual-token pruning and quantized KV-cache trade-offs for long-context video reasoning.
